# UK CleanTech Horizon Grant 2026 — Program Guidelines (Amended v2)

## 1. Eligibility Criteria
The lead applicant must be a UK-registered SME operating for at least 24 months with fewer than 150 employees. Academic institutions and non-UK entities are eligible only as secondary consortium partners.

## 2. Financial Documentation
Applications must include audited financial statements for the previous three financial years with signed balance sheets.

## 3. Technical & Environmental Impact
Projects must achieve a minimum 50% reduction in carbon emissions verified by third-party life-cycle analysis.

## 4. Commercial Partner Validation
Applicants must provide binding letters of commitment from at least three commercial pilot partners.
