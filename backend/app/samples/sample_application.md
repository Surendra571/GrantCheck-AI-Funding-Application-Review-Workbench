# Grant Application Draft — EcoFilter Clean Water Reclamation

## Section A: Company Profile & Eligibility
EcoFilter Ltd is a UK-registered micro-SME (Companies House #09876543) incorporated in March 2023, operating continuously for 30 months from our headquarters in Manchester, UK with 14 full-time employees.

## Section B: Project Objectives
Our project scales next-generation graphene-membrane filtration systems for industrial wastewater recycling. Our membrane technology reduces filtration energy consumption by 65% compared to all market alternatives.

## Section C: Environmental Impact
Our pilot data indicates an estimated 35-45% decrease in plant greenhouse emissions across operational trial sites during steady-state filtration cycles.

## Section D: Commercial Partners
Two letters of intent are attached from Yorkshire Water and Northumbrian Water plc confirming partner deployment in Phase 2.

## Section E: Financial Overview
